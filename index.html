MCAV · Drivetrain development

Wheel & Hub Development

02

I modelled an alloy wheel from workshop measurements of the physical wheel and tyre, then contributed to drivetrain refinements. The work included reducing mass, considering CNC manufacturability and adding a sealant provision in response to an observed oil leak.

  • Reverse engineering from measured parts
  • Weight-reduction features
  • CNC manufacturing considerations
  • Sealant interface for leak mitigation

MCAV onboarding · RC vehicle

RC Car Steering System

03

For the onboarding vehicle, I developed steering components within the complete RC car assembly. This included modelling the front CVD driveshaft from manufacturer dimensions, designing the steering-knuckle interfaces and applying bearing-fit tolerancing.

  • Steering knuckle and hub-carrier geometry
  • CVD driveshaft model from product dimensions
  • Bearing fits and tolerancing
  • Full-assembly clearance checks
