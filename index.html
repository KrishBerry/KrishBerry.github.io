01 / COMPETITION MECHANISM

Warman Capture & Deposit Mechanism

3rd place Warman Design & Build Competition

My first major CAD project focused on designing the robot’s object-capture and depositing mechanisms.

I developed the mechanism geometry, supported its integration with the complete team robot and contributed to prototype testing. The project introduced me to component interfaces, assembly constraints and design iteration.

MY CONTRIBUTION

Capture mechanism · Deposit mechanism · CAD development · Integration · Testing

PROTOTYPE TESTING

Capture and deposit sequence

Development testing was used to observe the mechanism’s movement, object handling and integration with the complete robot.

  2. 02

  5. 05

03 / ONBOARDING PROJECT

RC Car Steering System

Designed steering components from manufacturer information and integrated them into the complete RC vehicle assembly.

My work included modelling the steering shaft or universal component from supplied dimensions, designing its retaining coupling at the steering column and applying bearing-fit tolerances.

The completed steering assembly was checked for alignment, component clearance and steering movement.

DESIGN FOCUS

Drawing interpretation · Steering components · Coupling design · Bearing tolerances · Assembly integration
